Support FAQs

How long does shipping take?

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days depending on your location within India. You can track your order using the portal above once it has been dispatched.

What is your return & replacement policy?

We offer a hassle-free 7-Day Replacement Guarantee for any manufacturing defects or transit damage. Simply raise a ticket using the form above with your Order ID, and our team will arrange a free replacement.

Can I use a third-party power adapter?

We highly recommend using only the factory-provided power adapter. Using third-party adapters with incorrect voltage/amperage can permanently damage the RGBIC LEDs and will void the warranty.

My lights are stuck on one color, what should I do?

This is usually a sync issue. Check the "Hub Reset" step in the troubleshooting section above. If the issue persists, ensure all connections between the strip and controller are tight.
