Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to download an app to control the lights?

No! Our controller hub is fully Wi-Fi enabled. You can connect and control your lighting modes instantly from any web browser on your phone, tablet, or PC without taking up storage space.

How difficult is the installation process?

It's incredibly simple. The kit features a plug-and-play design with tool-free quick connectors. You can have your entire space lit up and synced in under 5 minutes.

How does the music sync feature work?

The controller handles AI music algorithms locally using its onboard processor and integrated microphone, meaning it reacts to your room's audio in real-time without needing to route sound through a secondary device.

Can I cut the LED strips to fit my specific setup?

Yes. The premium WS2812B strips have designated cut lines. You can trim them to fit your exact measurements for a custom desk or monitor setup without damaging the rest of the strip.
